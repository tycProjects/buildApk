📦 How to zip your project

      
Almost any Gradle/Android project zip works now — this is auto-detected and fixed up, not just checked. Here's what happens automatically, and the one thing to actually avoid.

      
    
        
  • 
          
    1
    
          
    Any nesting depth is fine. The zip's settings.gradle (or settings.gradle.kts) is found wherever it is — one top-level folder, a few levels deep, doesn't matter.
    
        
    • 
        
  • 
          
    2
    
          
    No Gradle wrapper? No problem — a runner-installed Gradle is used instead. Have one? It's used as-is, and common issues (missing execute permission, Windows CRLF line endings in gradlew) are fixed automatically.
    
        
    • 
        
  • 
          
    3
    
          
    A leftover local.properties pointing at your own machine's SDK path is removed automatically — it would otherwise break the build on the CI runner even though your project is fine.
    
        
    • 
        
  • 
          
    4
    
          
    Have a custom CI workflow? Include your own .github/workflows/*.yml with a workflow_dispatch trigger and it'll be used automatically. Otherwise a workflow that adapts to whatever your zip contains is generated for you.
    
        
    • 
        
  • 
          
    5
    
          
    Leave out build output — build/, .gradle/, *.apk — it's regenerated fresh and skipped automatically if included.
    
        
    • 
      


      


      

        

          Works fine
          MyApp.zip
└ MyApp/
  ├ settings.gradle
  ├ build.gradle
  └ app/
…nested further, wrapper or no wrapper.
        

        

          Avoid
          A zip with no settings.gradle anywhere in it — that's the one thing that can't be auto-detected.
